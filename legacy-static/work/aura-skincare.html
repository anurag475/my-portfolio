E-commerce

Aura Skincare — a storefront that makes premium skincare feel premium online.

A modern online store with a smooth product browsing and checkout experience, built to increase average order value and repeat purchases.

IndustryE-commerce / D2C Beauty
ServicesStore Design, Checkout Optimization
TechnologiesShopify, Custom Theme, Stripe
Timeline4 weeks
Challenge

A premium product, undercut by a generic storefront.

Aura's products were premium-priced, but their existing store used a generic free theme that made the brand look inconsistent with its pricing — and undercut customer trust right at the moment it mattered most: checkout.

Solution

A custom storefront designed to lift order value, not just look nicer.

We built a custom Shopify theme around large, clean product photography, a streamlined two-step checkout, and smart bundling prompts designed specifically to lift average order value without feeling pushy.

Design

Soft, editorial, and product-led.

Restrained color and typography let the product photography do the talking, with a sticky add-to-cart bar on mobile to reduce friction at the exact moment a visitor decides to buy.

Development

Merchandising flexibility, built in.

Custom Liquid sections gave the Aura team merchandising flexibility without developer help, Stripe handled payments securely, and Klaviyo was integrated for automated abandoned-cart recovery emails.

Result

Higher order values, fewer abandoned carts.

The relaunched store immediately felt more aligned with the brand's premium pricing — and the numbers followed within the first full sales cycle after launch.
