GreenRoots Foundation — making impact visible, and giving effortless.

A donation-ready awareness website that communicates impact clearly and makes it effortless for supporters to contribute.

IndustryNon-Profit / NGO
ServicesWebsite Build, Donation Integration
TechnologiesWordPress, Custom Theme, Razorpay
Timeline3 weeks
Challenge

A powerful cause, held back by a static brochure.

GreenRoots relied on a static PDF brochure and a single email address to receive donations — a serious barrier for supporters who wanted to give quickly, and a missed opportunity to tell their story at scale.

Solution

A website that leads with impact, not paperwork.

We built a warm, transparent website that leads with impact stories and outcomes, backed by a one-click, secure online donation flow designed to remove every unnecessary step between intent and contribution.

Design

Calm, transparent, and trustworthy.

Photography-led storytelling sections, a transparent "where your money goes" breakdown, and a calm color palette suited to a cause-driven organization — designed to build confidence, not urgency-based pressure tactics.

Development

Built for a team without developers on staff.

The site runs on WordPress specifically so GreenRoots' own team can publish new stories and updates without needing a developer, with Razorpay integrated for secure one-time and recurring donations.

Result

More donations, and more of them recurring.

Within the first quarter after launch, online giving became GreenRoots' primary donation channel — with a growing share of supporters choosing to give monthly rather than once.
