Professional Services

LawLine Associates — a digital presence as credible as the practice itself.

A polished, trust-building website for a legal practice, designed to make new client enquiries simple and credible.

IndustryLegal / Professional Services
ServicesWebsite Build, Practice Area Pages, SEO
TechnologiesNext.js, Headless CMS
Timeline3 weeks
Challenge

Visitors bounced before ever finding a contact form.

LawLine's previous website looked outdated and didn't clearly explain their practice areas, causing potential clients to bounce before ever reaching a contact form — a costly problem for a firm that depends on referral trust.

Solution

Structure that builds credibility page by page.

We designed a structured, credibility-first website with dedicated practice area pages, attorney profiles and a short, low-friction enquiry form — so every page answers a specific question a prospective client would ask.

Design

Serious, restrained, and never a stock-photo cliché.

A restrained, editorial design system using serif headlines and generous whitespace to convey seriousness and trustworthiness, deliberately avoiding the generic "corporate stock photo" look common in the industry.

Development

Built so the firm can publish without a developer.

Built with Next.js and a headless CMS so the firm can publish articles and update attorney profiles without developer involvement, with technical SEO — clean markup, sitemaps, structured data — built in from the start.

Result

More enquiries, and visibility where it counts.

The new site didn't just look better — it started ranking, and it started converting visitors who previously would have left without ever making contact.
