Restaurant

Spice Route Kitchen — from word of mouth to online reservations.

A mouth-watering restaurant website with an online menu, table reservations and location details that drive footfall.

IndustryRestaurant / Hospitality
ServicesWebsite Build, Reservation System, Local SEO
TechnologiesHTML/CSS/JS, Node.js, Google Maps
Timeline2 weeks
Challenge

No website meant no direct customers.

Spice Route had no website at all — customers found them only through word of mouth or third-party delivery apps that took a large commission on every single order, cutting directly into their margins.

Solution

A website that puts the menu and a table one tap away.

We built a fast, visually rich website showcasing the menu and ambience, with a built-in table reservation form and clear directions — reducing reliance on delivery platforms and their commissions.

Design

Warm, appetite-driving, and easy to scan.

A photography-forward layout, an easy-to-scan digital menu organized by course, and prominent reservation calls-to-action placed throughout the page rather than buried in a single "contact" tab.

Development

Lightweight, fast, and location-aware.

A lightweight custom build with a Node.js backend for handling reservations directly, plus Google Maps integration for directions — optimized specifically for fast mobile load during "restaurants near me" searches.

Result

Real bookings, straight from Google search.

Within two months, the website had become a genuine reservation channel in its own right, with the majority of new visitors arriving directly from local search rather than delivery apps or referrals.
