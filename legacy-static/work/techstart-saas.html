SaaS Startup

TechStart — turning a beta product into a fundable, credible SaaS brand.

A conversion-focused marketing site and pricing funnel for an early-stage SaaS startup preparing for public launch and its first funding round.

IndustrySaaS / Startup
ServicesLanding Page, Pricing Funnel, Analytics
TechnologiesReact, Tailwind CSS, Vercel
Timeline3 weeks
Challenge

No credible home for a product people were already asking about.

TechStart had a working product in private beta but no public-facing website — investors and early users had nowhere credible to learn about the company before their upcoming launch. Their existing one-page document undermined the professionalism the product itself displayed.

Solution

A marketing site built around one goal: qualified demo requests.

We designed and built a fast, focused marketing site around a single conversion path: getting qualified visitors onto a demo call or free trial, backed by a clear pricing page and a guided product walkthrough that answered objections before they were asked.

Design

Confident, product-first — not another generic startup gradient.

The visual system leans on a dark hero, generous whitespace and sharp typography, letting real product screenshots do the persuading instead of decorative illustration or overused purple gradients.

Development

Built for speed, from the first commit.

The site was built with React and Tailwind CSS for fast iteration during the pre-launch sprint, deployed to Vercel's edge network, and instrumented with lightweight, privacy-friendly analytics from day one so the team could see what was working.

Result

More demo requests, from day one.

Within 30 days of switching from their placeholder document to the new site, TechStart saw a measurable lift in qualified demo requests — with a site fast enough to keep even skeptical, technical visitors around.
